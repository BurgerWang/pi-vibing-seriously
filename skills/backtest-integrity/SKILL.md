# Backtest Integrity

Goal: demonstrate that a backtest measures the strategy and only the
strategy. A backtest with leakage or misaligned execution measures an
imaginary strategy; every result must survive the checks below before it is
reported.

## Core checks

1. **Future leakage** — no information from after a bar's timestamp is
   used in that bar's decision. Audit the data flow: features, filters,
   normalization, and universe membership.
2. **Look-ahead bias** — the subtler form of leakage: restated data,
   full-sample statistics, survivor lists, and corporate actions applied
   with knowledge of the future.
3. **Signal/execution alignment** — the execution price must come from
   AFTER the signal timestamp, under the documented convention (next
   open, next close). Trading at the signal price is the most common
   silent error.
4. **Adjustments** — split/dividend-adjusted prices used consistently;
   total-return vs price-return stated (see `skill:market-data-integrity`).
5. **Suspensions** — a suspended name cannot be traded: the backtest must
   define what happens to holdings and signals during suspension.
6. **Delisting** — delisted names must exit at a documented final price,
   not disappear silently (see `skill:market-data-integrity`).
7. **Fees and slippage** — the cost model (commission/fee rates, slippage
   per unit of traded volume) is explicit and applied to every trade.
8. **Cash and positions** — every unit of capital is accounted for:
   cash earns the stated rate, positions are marked at the stated price,
   and fractional-share or rounding rules are documented.
9. **Benchmark alignment** — benchmark returns computed on the same dates,
   prices, and conventions as the strategy.
10. **Return computation** — compounding, period aggregation, and
    per-period returns match their definitions; returns reconcile with the
    position marks.
11. **Rebalance semantics** — the trade list (what is bought/sold, at what
    price, when) is generated by the stated rule and executed with the
    stated delay.

## Out of scope

This skill covers mid/low-frequency backtesting correctness. It does not
cover order book microstructure, tick replay, queue models, market making,
colocation, microsecond latency work, or exchange execution engines — none
of which are part of this workbench's scope.

## Rules

- Every backtest reports its conventions (price, timing, costs, universe)
  before its numbers.
- A check that cannot be verified is reported as NOT_RUN, never assumed.
- When a leak is found, the affected results are invalid until re-run —
  patching the report without re-running is not acceptable.
